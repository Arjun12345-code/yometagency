Earnings Withdrawal

Yomet Withdrawal Information and Tutorial

Learn how to withdraw your Yomet earnings, understand the payment schedule, and complete the process.

Minimum Withdrawal

Minimum withdrawal: $10 (10,000 Beans). Requests must be in multiples of $10.

Bean Accumulation

Unused Beans accumulate for future withdrawals.

Settlement Schedule

Settlement runs Monday to Sunday. Payments are processed every Monday at 10:00 AM (UTC+7).

Commission Formula

Sub-agency commission formula:
(Your daily ratio - Sub-agent daily ratio) × Sub-agent daily revenue.

Agency Onboarding

How to Register as a Yomet Agent

Getting started with Yomet Agency is simple. Follow these steps to register and activate your agency account.

1

Open the agency registration link.

Use the official Yomet/Olamet agency registration link here.

If the page displays “We invite you to be the host of Yomet,” this is not a problem as long as you entered through the link.
2

Enter your active phone number.

Make sure your phone number is active

3

Verify your phone number.

Click “Send” to receive the OTP. If the code does not arrive, try again after a few minutes.

4

Download the Yomet/Olamet app.

Search for “Olamet” on the Google Play Store.

5

Log in with your registered number.

Use the same phone number you registered with

6

Start building your team.

Once the process is complete, your agency account is ready.
